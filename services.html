Maintenance

Priced separately, never buried

You will always see two numbers: the build, and the upkeep. The upkeep is shown as a monthly or annual rate with an explicit start date, and it appears on the agreement before you sign.

It covers uptime monitoring, verified backups (we test-restore one, monthly), dependency and security updates, small content changes within an agreed limit, and a monthly email report.

Not included

What we will tell you upfront

  • Product photography and videography
  • Ad spend on Google or Meta
  • Writing your product descriptions beyond the agreed SKU count
  • Payment gateway fees charged by the bank or aggregator
  • Domain and hosting fees, paid at cost and in your name
  • Courier charges and courier-side delays
  • Legal, tax or GST filing advice

Straight answers

Questions we get before signing

Who owns the website when it is done?

You do. Admin credentials, database, hosting account and the domain transfer to you on final payment. It is written into the agreement.

Can I move to another developer later?

Yes. That is the point of the handover. You leave with the code, the data and the domain — nothing is held back.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the band and how ready your product data is. The agreement states a delivery date, and we do not start the clock until the form is complete.

What if I only want a catalogue, no online payment?

That is a valid setup and gets a smaller scope. Say so on the form — we would rather build what you will actually use.

Do you work across Rajasthan?

Yes — all over Rajasthan. We are based in Jaipur and visit shops where a visit helps; where it does not, we work remotely and confirm the real workflow on a call before we build.

Is my data safe with you?

The database is provisioned in your name from day one, access is logged, and we publish what we do and do not do with your data on the terms page.
