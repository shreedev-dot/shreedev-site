How the demo works

A demo is your shop, not a template tour

When we reach out to a business, we do not send a generic portfolio. We pick the demo closest to their category, rename it to a neutral placeholder, and load it with products that a shop like theirs would actually sell.

Your own version goes further: your logo, your products, your delivery rules, your invoice format. You approve a staging link before anything is charged.

About the images.

Demo imagery uses openly licensed stock photography chosen to match the category, with the licence recorded per image. We never lift photos from a business’s Google listing or social page, and never publish a shop’s own photos before they say yes in writing.
