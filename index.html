How it works

Three conversations, no surprises

Days 0–2

We show you your own store first

Before you pay anything, we send a demo built for your kind of business — your category, your products, your city — within two working days. You look at it on your own phone.

Days 3–7

You pick what you need

A short form, pre-filled with what we already know about your shop, with optional extras. Your answers set the scope and produce an estimate — no haggling over a mystery number.

Weeks 1–8, by band

We build, you sign, we hand over

A plain-language agreement with the final price, your delivery date and the maintenance terms. On final payment you get the admin login, the database, the hosting and the domain — in your name.

Before we start

What we need from you

Five things, and the build can start. The clock starts when your content is complete — your delivery date is in the agreement.

  • Product list with prices
  • Photos
  • GSTIN
  • Delivery/shipping rules
  • Logo, if you have one

The handover rule

Full access transfers after the final payment clears. Before that, you get a staging link to review. The domain is registered in your name, so you are never held hostage by us.

Why we put this in writing on the homepage.

Because the usual complaint about web developers is that the shop owner ends up renting their own website. Admin credentials, database ownership, hosting and DNS all move to you at handover — and if you ever want to leave, you take the site with you.
